## Pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does ReviewBox cost?">
    Three plans:

    - **Starter** — $49/mo · 1 app · 250 AI calls/mo · email support
    - **Pro** — $99/mo · 3 apps · 2,000 AI calls/mo · automations, knowledge
      base, priority support
    - **Team** — $199/mo · 10 apps · unlimited AI calls · auto-reply, team
      seats, custom tone

    All plans include a 14-day free trial — no card required up front.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer annual plans?">
    Yes, annual plans save 20%. Contact
    [hello@tryreviewbox.com](mailto:hello@tryreviewbox.com) to switch — we'll
    swap your subscription and prorate the difference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans later?">
    Anytime. **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades take effect
    immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when the trial ends?">
    Your workspace becomes read-only — you can view reviews and replies but
    you can't sync new reviews or publish replies until you pick a plan. No
    data is deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you have a free plan?">
    Not currently. The 14-day trial is meant to give you enough time to
    decide. Solo developers with a single small app can email us — we
    occasionally grant extended trials.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Supported stores

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which app stores do you support?">
    - **Google Play** — full support: review sync, AI triage, reply
      publishing
    - **Apple App Store** — full support: review sync, AI triage, reply
      publishing (via App Store Connect API)
    - Other stores (Amazon, Galaxy Store, Huawei) — not currently on the
      roadmap
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How recent are the reviews you sync?">
    On first connect, ReviewBox pulls the last 60 days. After that, a sync
    runs every 4 hours and pulls anything new since the last run. Manual
    sync is available under **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sync historical reviews older than 60 days?">
    Not currently. Google Play's API only exposes the most recent week
    reliably — we extend coverage by deduping across syncs and storing
    everything we've ever seen, so over time your history grows. App Store
    Connect exposes more history; we backfill what's available on connect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security and privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How are my Google Play / App Store credentials stored?">
    Encrypted at rest in our database, with separate access controls from
    review data. Credentials are only decrypted in-memory when a sync runs
    and are never logged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What permissions does ReviewBox require?">
    The minimum needed: **View app information** and **Reply to reviews**.
    Nothing else. We never request access to financials, releases, or any
    write capabilities outside the reply scope.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    PostgreSQL hosted on Supabase (US East and EU West regions). All data is
    encrypted at rest and in transit. Reviews data is segregated per
    workspace using row-level security.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you use my data to train AI models?">
    No. Reviews and replies are never used to train third-party models.
    We use Groq (Llama 3.3) for inference, and per their policy, prompts
    aren't retained.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are you SOC 2 / GDPR compliant?">
    GDPR: yes — we offer data export and deletion under **Settings → Privacy**
    and via [hello@tryreviewbox.com](mailto:hello@tryreviewbox.com). SOC 2:
    we're targeting Type 1 in 2026 H2. Enterprise customers can request our
    current security overview.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI and replies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which AI model do you use?">
    Llama 3.3 70B via Groq for live drafts. We're moving heavier workspaces
    to Claude Haiku 3.5 in 2026 — no change to your experience, just
    higher-quality replies at similar latency.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will AI replies sound generic?">
    The knowledge base and reply tones are designed to prevent that. Loading
    even 3–4 knowledge base entries about your product makes a dramatic
    difference. Workspaces on the Team plan can request a custom tone built
    from samples of their existing replies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I auto-reply without reviewing each draft?">
    Yes, on the Team plan. Auto-reply is opt-in per automation rule. We
    recommend running rules in auto-draft mode for 1–2 weeks before flipping
    them to auto-reply.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages do you support?">
    AI drafts in 12 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese,
    Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (simplified), Arabic, and
    Hindi. Reply templates can be written in any language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my AI reply get flagged?">
    We run safety checks on every draft. The most common flags are: quoting
    a price not in your knowledge base, promising a refund, or claiming a
    fix ETA. Drafts that trip these checks are returned with a warning and
    a sanitized version. Hit **Report this draft** if you ever see one
    that's wrong.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and team

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite teammates?">
    Yes. On Pro and Team plans, invite via **Settings → Team**. Pro includes
    3 seats, Team includes 10. Each member sees the same workspace data and
    shared knowledge base.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. You keep full access until
    the end of the current billing period, then drop to read-only. Your data
    is preserved for 90 days in case you want to come back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Email [hello@tryreviewbox.com](mailto:hello@tryreviewbox.com) from your
    account email and we'll permanently delete your workspace and all
    associated data within 7 days, per our GDPR commitment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my data?">
    Yes. **Settings → Privacy → Export data** generates a ZIP with all your
    reviews, replies, templates, knowledge base, and automation rules in
    JSON. Available on every plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My reply didn't appear on the store">
    Google Play replies typically take 1–4 hours to surface publicly. App
    Store Connect replies can take up to 24 hours. If a reply still isn't
    visible after that window, check the review's status in ReviewBox — a
    red badge indicates an API error we couldn't auto-recover.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reviews stopped syncing">
    Check **Settings → Integrations**. If you see "Auth failed", rotate your
    service account key (Google Play) or API key (App Store Connect) and
    re-paste the new credentials.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm getting too many rating spike alerts">
    Adjust the threshold under **Settings → Alerts**. Default is 5 reviews
    rated 1–2★ on the same version in 24 hours — you can raise the count or
    extend the window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI is missing context about a known bug">
    Add it to the knowledge base under **Reply Kit → Knowledge Base**.
    Future drafts that mention the bug will reference your fix info
    automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
