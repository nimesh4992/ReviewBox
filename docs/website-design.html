What ReviewBox does

Review Management
Both stores, every market, one queue sorted by what actually needs your attention — not by when it arrived.
  • Syncs every 4h automatically. No plugins, no browser extensions.
  • Priority: crash → billing → UX regression → feature request
  • Filter by version, country, rating, or sentiment in one click
AI Replies
Template-matched first. KB-grounded always. Sounds like you because it references your product knowledge, not a generic prompt.
  • ~3 seconds end-to-end including knowledge base lookup
  • Publishes directly to Google Play and App Store Connect

Incident Detection
ReviewBox watches every sync for crash clusters and rating spikes. You get an email before your PM finds out from Slack.

ASO Intelligence
