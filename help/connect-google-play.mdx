ReviewBox uses the official [Google Play Android Publisher
API](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher) — the same API Google
recommends for partner integrations. You'll create a Google Cloud service
account, grant it limited access to your Play Console, and paste the
credentials into ReviewBox.

The full process takes about 5 minutes.

## What you'll need

- Admin access to your Google Play Console
- Permission to create resources in a Google Cloud project (your own or your
  organization's)

## Step 1 — Create or pick a Google Cloud project

1. Go to the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com)
2. Click the project picker in the top bar
3. Either **New Project** or select an existing one your team uses for app
   tooling

Make a note of the project ID — you'll need it in the next step.

## Step 2 — Enable the Android Publisher API

1. In Cloud Console, open **APIs & Services → Library**
2. Search for **Google Play Android Developer API**
3. Click **Enable**

<Note>
  This is a free Google API. There is no per-call charge for review fetch or
  reply operations.
</Note>

## Step 3 — Create a service account

1. Open **APIs & Services → Credentials**
2. **Create credentials → Service account**
3. Name it something like `reviewbox-sync`
4. Skip the optional role grants — we'll handle permissions in Play Console
5. Click **Done**

Open the new service account and go to the **Keys** tab:

1. **Add key → Create new key**
2. Choose **JSON**
3. Download and keep the file safe — Google will only show this key once

You'll paste two values from this JSON into ReviewBox:

- `client_email`
- `private_key`

## Step 4 — Grant access in Play Console

1. Open [Play Console → Users and permissions](https://play.google.com/console/users-and-permissions)
2. **Invite new user**
3. Paste the service account's `client_email` as the email address
4. Under **App permissions**, add the apps you want ReviewBox to manage
5. For each app, grant:
   - **View app information and download bulk reports**
   - **Reply to reviews**
6. Click **Invite user**

<Warning>
  Don't grant **Admin (all permissions)** to the service account. ReviewBox
  only needs the two permissions above. Anything broader exposes more than
  necessary.
</Warning>

## Step 5 — Paste credentials into ReviewBox

1. In ReviewBox, open **Settings → Integrations**
2. Click **Connect Google Play**
3. Paste the `client_email` and `private_key` from the JSON file
4. Enter the package name for each app you want to sync (e.g.
   `com.yourcompany.yourapp`)
5. Click **Save and sync**

The first sync pulls reviews from the last 60 days. You'll see them appear in
the **Reviews** tab within a few minutes.

## How sync works after setup

After the first sync, ReviewBox automatically re-syncs every 4 hours. Each
sync:

1. Fetches new reviews from Google Play
2. Runs them through the AI triage pipeline (sentiment, priority, tags)
3. Checks rate spike thresholds and fires an email alert if triggered
4. Auto-drafts replies for any reviews matching your saved automation rules

You can also trigger a manual sync from **Settings → Integrations → Sync now**.

## Troubleshooting

**"Permission denied" on first sync**
Wait 5–10 minutes. Google Play permissions can take a few minutes to
propagate after inviting the service account.

**Reviews stopped syncing**
Open **Settings → Integrations**. If you see a red "Auth failed" badge, the
service account key may have been rotated. Generate a new key in Cloud
Console and re-paste it.

**Some apps aren't appearing**
Confirm the service account was granted access to those specific apps in
Play Console — not just the developer account. Per-app permissions are
required.

**Reply didn't appear on the store**
Replies typically take 1–4 hours to surface publicly. If a reply still hasn't
appeared after 24 hours, check **Reviews → [the review]** for an error
status, then contact us.
