Welcome to ReviewBox. This guide walks you through the first run — from sign-up
to publishing your first AI-assisted reply.

## What you'll need

- A Google Play Console account with at least one published app *(App Store
  Connect support is rolling out — see the [FAQ](/faq))*
- 5–10 minutes
- Your team's preferred reply tone (you can change this later)

## 1. Create your account

Head to [app.tryreviewbox.com/sign-up](https://app.tryreviewbox.com/sign-up)
and sign up with email or Google. You'll be taken straight to your dashboard.

<Tip>
  ReviewBox is free for 14 days — no card required. You won't be asked for
  billing details until you decide to keep going.
</Tip>

## 2. Set up your workspace

The first time you log in, a setup card appears with two fields:

1. **Workspace name** — usually your company or team name
2. **App name** — what your team calls the app internally

Your workspace URL slug is generated automatically from the workspace name.
You can rename either field later under **Settings → Workspace**.

## 3. Connect Google Play

Click **Connect Google Play** on the dashboard. We walk you through:

1. Creating a service account in Google Cloud
2. Granting the service account permission in Play Console
3. Pasting the credentials into ReviewBox

Step-by-step instructions live in
[Connect Google Play](/connect-google-play). It takes about 5 minutes the
first time.

<Note>
  ReviewBox stores Google Play credentials encrypted at rest. We only request
  the minimum permissions needed to read reviews and post replies.
</Note>

## 4. Watch your first sync

Within a few minutes of connecting, ReviewBox pulls your last 60 days of
reviews and runs them through the AI triage pipeline:

- **Sentiment** — critical, negative, mixed, or positive
- **Priority** — urgent, high, normal, or low
- **Issue tags** — crash, billing, login, performance, feature request, and more

The sidebar shows a live count of reviews waiting on you. After the initial
backfill, ReviewBox re-syncs automatically every 4 hours.

## 5. Reply to a review

Open the **Reviews** tab in the sidebar. Each card shows the review text,
rating, device, country, and the AI-detected tags.

To reply:

1. Click any review card to expand it
2. Hit **Draft with AI** — a reply is generated in 1–2 seconds
3. Edit if you'd like, then click **Publish**

The reply is posted to Google Play via the official API. It typically appears
on the store within 1–4 hours.

<Tip>
  Set up reply templates and a knowledge base under **Reply Kit** for faster,
  more on-brand replies. See [AI Replies](/ai-replies) for details.
</Tip>

## 6. Configure alerts

Under **Settings → Alerts**, choose when ReviewBox should email you:

- Rating spike — 5+ reviews rated 1–2★ on the same app version in 24h
- Unreplied reminders — reviews sitting more than 48 hours
- Weekly digest — summary of last week's review activity, every Monday morning
