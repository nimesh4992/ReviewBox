The Problem

Reviews pile up.
Ratings drop.
You find out last.

Mobile teams are flying blind — reviews land faster than anyone can read, crashes go unacknowledged, and every unanswered 1-star drags your store ranking down.

  • ⚡

    You miss the critical ones

    Crash reports and billing complaints get buried in routine feedback. By the time you find them, the damage is done.

  • ⏱

    Replying takes forever

    Open the store console, read context, write a thoughtful reply — repeat 40 times a week. It's a job nobody hired for.

  • 📉

    Rating drops blindside you

    A bad release ships. Ratings spike downward. You find out three days later when the CEO notices the score changed.

Features

Everything your
review operation needs.

One tool that watches both stores, triages every review, drafts replies in your voice, and pings you before a bad release becomes a crisis.

Auto-sync

🧠

AI triage

Reply drafts

How it works

Set up in minutes.
Running in hours.

01

Connect your stores

Link Google Play via a service account. App Store connects via App Store Connect API keys. One-time, under 5 minutes.

02

Reviews sync automatically

ReviewBox pulls new reviews every 4 hours and enriches each one with sentiment, priority, and issue tags on arrival.

03

AI drafts replies for you

Click any review to see a ready-to-publish AI draft grounded in your knowledge base and tuned to your brand voice.

Rating spikes, crash clusters, and unreplied reviews trigger alerts before they become incidents. You always know first.
