ReviewBox uses a three-tier reply pipeline that's optimized for speed, cost,
and brand consistency. This page explains how it works and how to make the
output sound exactly like your team.

## The 3-tier pipeline

When you click **Draft with AI** on a review, ReviewBox tries each tier in
order and stops at the first match:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Template match">
    If the review's tag and rating match one of your saved reply templates,
    ReviewBox uses that template directly. Zero AI calls, instant response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cache hit">
    If a similar review has been replied to in the last 7 days, the cached
    reply is served. Replies are keyed by a SHA-256 hash of the compressed
    review text, so near-duplicates share a cached draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live generation">
    Otherwise, ReviewBox compresses the review (~30 filler phrases stripped,
    73% fewer tokens) and sends it to the AI provider with your knowledge
    base context and chosen tone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Most workspaces see 60–80% of drafts served from tiers 1 or 2, which is why
ReviewBox stays inside the AI free tier well past launch.

## Reply tones

Pick a default tone under **Reply Kit → AI Styles**. Four built-in styles
ship with every workspace:

| Style | When to use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Professional** | Default for most B2B apps | "Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Our engineering team is actively investigating this issue and a fix will be released shortly." |
| **Empathetic** | Apps with personal/emotional users (health, finance, dating) | "We're really sorry this is happening — that sounds incredibly frustrating. Our team is on it and we'll have this fixed as soon as possible." |
| **Direct** | Power-user apps where short is better | "Sorry for the crash. Fix shipping next update. Email hello@tryreviewbox.com if urgent." |
| **Casual** | Consumer apps with a playful brand | "Ugh, so sorry about that! We know crashes are the worst. Our team is already on it — update should be out soon. Hang tight!" |

You can override the default style per-reply with the dropdown in the draft
editor.

<Tip>
  Send us a sample of 5–10 hand-written replies and we'll build you a custom
  tone that mirrors your voice. Email
  [hello@tryreviewbox.com](mailto:hello@tryreviewbox.com).
</Tip>

## Knowledge base

The knowledge base teaches ReviewBox about your product so drafts don't
hallucinate facts. Under **Reply Kit → Knowledge Base**, add entries in
four categories:

- **Known issues** — current bugs, workarounds, ETAs for fixes
- **Product** — what the app does, key features, positioning
- **FAQs** — pricing, cancellation, supported platforms
- **Roadmap** — upcoming features users frequently ask about

<Note>
  Keep entries short and factual. ReviewBox includes the most relevant
  knowledge base snippets in each prompt — long entries crowd out the
  signal.
</Note>

**Example known issue entry:**

> v5.42.0 crash on Android 14: fix ships in v5.42.1 (ETA: Monday). Login
> loop on Samsung S-series: workaround is clearing cache.

When a reviewer mentions a v5.42.0 crash, that entry surfaces and the draft
references the fix timing automatically.

## Reply templates

Templates are exact-match replies that bypass AI entirely. Use them when:

- You always reply the same way to a specific tag + rating combination
- You need legally-reviewed wording for billing or refund replies
- You want to keep AI tokens free for the harder cases

Each template defines:

- A **tag filter** (e.g. `crash`, `billing`, `feature-request`)
- A **rating range** (e.g. 1–2 stars)
- A **language** (English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and more)
- The **reply body**

Templates ship pre-loaded with sensible defaults — edit or replace them under
**Reply Kit → Templates**.

## Auto-draft (automation rules)

Save trips into the dashboard by drafting replies automatically. Under
**Automations**, create rules like:

- "All 5-star reviews tagged `satisfied-user` → auto-draft with **Casual**
  style"
- "All `crash` reviews on v5.42.x → auto-draft with **Empathetic** style,
  reference the current known issue"

Auto-drafted replies sit in **draft_ready** status — a human still clicks
**Publish**. Workspaces on the Team plan can opt into **auto-reply** for
specific rules (no human in the loop).

<Warning>
  Auto-reply is opt-in per rule and per workspace. We strongly recommend
  starting in auto-draft mode for 1–2 weeks and reviewing the queue before
  enabling auto-reply.
</Warning>

## What the AI does *not* do

- It never invents a refund, a release date, or a contact promise that isn't
  in your knowledge base
- It never quotes pricing — drafts route the user to your pricing page or
  support email instead
- It will not reply in a language you haven't enabled for that template
- It will not exceed 120 words

If a draft ever crosses a line, click **Report this draft** in the editor.
We review every report and tighten the prompt.
